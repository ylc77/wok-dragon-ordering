# Client Legal Checklist

This project includes a basic legal pages template. It is not a substitute for advice from a lawyer, accountant or local compliance professional.

Before each client goes live, confirm and fill in the following information.

## Business identity

- [ ] businessName
- [ ] legalName
- [ ] businessAddress
- [ ] vatNumber
- [ ] gemiNumber
- [ ] country
- [ ] phone
- [ ] contactEmail

## Data controller

- [ ] dataControllerName
- [ ] dataControllerAddress
- [ ] Confirm who answers customer privacy requests
- [ ] Confirm how customers can request correction or deletion where legally available

## Service providers

Only list services that are actually enabled for this client.

- [ ] dataProcessors, for example Supabase and Vercel if used
- [ ] paymentProviders, for example cash, card terminal, Viva, Stripe or other provider only if enabled
- [ ] analyticsProviders, for example PostHog or Sentry only if enabled
- [ ] aiProviders, for example DeepSeek or OpenAI only if enabled
- [ ] Confirm non-essential cookies are not loaded before consent

## Retention and operations

- [ ] dataRetention wording
- [ ] lastUpdated date
- [ ] Confirm order records retention expectations
- [ ] Confirm image upload and storage usage
- [ ] Confirm staff/admin access responsibilities

## Restaurant project sections

- [ ] Order Terms
- [ ] Cancellation Policy
- [ ] Payment Terms
- [ ] Allergy / food availability disclaimer
- [ ] Confirm kitchen receipts are not official tax receipts
- [ ] Confirm official receipts are issued by the restaurant POS/cash register/accounting process

## Clothing / retail project sections

- [ ] Shipping Policy
- [ ] Return Policy
- [ ] Refund Policy
- [ ] 14-day withdrawal right section
- [ ] Return address and return shipping responsibility
- [ ] Excluded items, hygiene products, custom products or sale items

## Footer and submission notices

- [ ] Footer links are visible on public pages
- [ ] Privacy Policy link works
- [ ] Terms of Service link works
- [ ] Cookie Policy link works
- [ ] Contact link works
- [ ] Refund or Cancellation Policy link works
- [ ] Form submission notice is visible where customers submit information

## Final client confirmation

- [ ] Client reviewed business identity
- [ ] Client reviewed payment wording
- [ ] Client reviewed delivery / cancellation / refund wording
- [ ] Client confirmed enabled third-party services
- [ ] Client confirmed legal pages are basic legal pages template, not a fully customized legal review
